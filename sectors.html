Sector Expertise

Industry-Specific Security Tailored to Your Environment

Every sector faces distinct security challenges. MAN Commercial Protection delivers custom operating procedures, dedicated staff training, and compliance frameworks to safeguard every industry environment.

Logistics & Distribution

Protecting national fulfillment hubs, distribution depots, and multi-acre transport yards. We manage 24/7 gatehouses, weighbridges, trailer seal checks, driver inductions, and supply chain loss prevention.

Key Services: Gatehouse Security • Trailer Seal Audits • Yard Marshaling • ANPR

Corporate & Commercial

Seamlessly integrating corporate concierge, customer care, and access control for city centre office high-rises, headquarters, and business parks with polished presentation.

Key Services: Front of House Concierge • Visitor Badging • Executive Escort • Lock/Unlock

Marine, Ports & Luxury Shipyards

Trusted security partner to elite marine brands including Sunseeker International for over 8 years, protecting high-value superyacht construction yards, deep water berths, and VIP client assets.

Key Services: Shipyard Access Control • Berthing Security • International VIP Protection • Cash Carrying

Manufacturing & Industrial

Strict health and safety compliance, perimeter deterrence, contractor inductions, and hazardous materials checkpoint security for production facilities, automotive plants, and heavy manufacturing sites.

Key Services: Health & Safety Gatekeeping • Perimeter CCTV • Out-of-Hours Patrols

Retail & Shopping Parks

Protecting retail staff, stock, and shoppers. We deliver uniformed visual deterrence, anti-social behavior mitigation, store detective loss prevention, and rapid incident response for shopping centres.

Key Services: Uniformed Floor Guards • Store Detectives • First Aid Response • CCTV Coordination

Public Sector, NHS & Infrastructure

Delivering high-compliance, DBS-checked security personnel for local authorities, healthcare trusts, government civic centers, and educational campuses across the UK.

Key Services: Enhanced DBS Officers • Conflict Management • De-escalation • Keyholding
