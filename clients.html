Featured Marine Case Study • 8+ Years Partnership

Sunseeker International Superyacht Security

Sunseeker International is the world-renowned British luxury motor yacht manufacturer based in Poole, Dorset. MAN Commercial Protection has proudly delivered 24/7 security services across Sunseeker’s extensive shipyard facilities for over eight years.

Our responsibilities encompass perimeter monitoring across deep-water berths, high-value asset safeguarding, shipyard access control, international VIP client protection, and cash-in-transit security.

"The MAN Commercial team at West Quay Road are professional, diligent, and provide a seamless security service. They have looked after everything from our manufacturing facilities to high-profile VIP client events."
— Alexandra Harrison, Group PR & Marketing Director, Sunseeker London
Strategic Growth & Corporate Partnership

The CFO Centre Success Story

As MAN Commercial Protection scaled rapidly from a regional provider into a multi-million-pound national security leader, we partnered with The CFO Centre to structure strategic financial planning, capital expansion, and operational investments.

This partnership enabled our investment into our freehold Solihull headquarters, our Category 2 NSI Gold National Operations Control Centre, and our in-house Training Academy.

Key Operational Milestone: Expanded national coverage across 1,500+ security officers while maintaining 100% independent private ownership.
